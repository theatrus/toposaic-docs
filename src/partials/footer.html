Data & attribution

TopoSaic draws on public map data. Publicly shared prints, images, and files must keep the data-source credits recorded in each manifest, or place them near the work.

Elevation: Mapzen Terrain Tiles & Mapterhorn · Land cover: ESA WorldCover · Roads, railways, lifts, water, buildings, and place search: OpenStreetMap via Overpass & Nominatim.
